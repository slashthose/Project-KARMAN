WHAT IS PROJECT KARMAN?

Turning Informal Skills into Certified Careers

More than 90% of India's workforce and millions of graduating youth hold rich, practical skills that lack formal accredited documentation. Project KARMAN solves this nationwide challenge through AI-driven competency extraction and NSQF alignment.

🎯

AI Skill Gap & ATS Engine

Upload resumes or speak your background to calculate instant ATS readiness scores, matching percentage, and step-by-step gap closing targets.

🏛️

NSQF Trade Alignment

Automated mapping of informal coursework and field work to official National Skills Qualification Framework occupational trades (Levels 1 to 8).

🗣️

Multilingual Voice Bot

Integrated with WhatsApp Voice (+1-555-203-7186) and web chat in Hindi, English, and regional languages for barrier-free accessibility.

Govt Scheme Matcher

Direct linkage with PMKVY 4.0, PM-AJAY equipment grants, and Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) certification with tool-kit stipends.
